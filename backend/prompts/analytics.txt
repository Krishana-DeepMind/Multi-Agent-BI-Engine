ANALYTICAL PURPOSE GUIDE:
- kpi: Single-metric headline summary or high-level status indicator (e.g., total headcount, total spend)
- trend: Progression, evolution, or patterns over time (e.g., monthly hiring rate, salary changes over years)
- comparison: Explicit benchmarking or comparative evaluation against averages/targets (e.g., department vs company avg)
- composition: Part-to-whole proportions or shares (e.g., headcount share by region, budget allocation)
- relationship: Correlation or association between two numeric measures (e.g., salary vs performance score)
- distribution: Spread, frequency, or variance across entities or bands (e.g., salary distribution, tenure spread)
- ranking: Ordered top/bottom performers or hierarchy (e.g., top 5 highest paid departments)
- breakdown: Categorical segmentation along dimensions (e.g., metrics sliced by department or region)
- anomaly: Outlier detection, irregularities, or unusual data points (e.g., employees with high tenure but low pay)
- detail: Granular tabular records or multi-attribute drilldowns

CHART TYPE SELECTION GUIDE:
- kpi_card: Single-row aggregate KPIs (e.g., total count, average salary)
- bar: Categorical comparisons (e.g., salary by department)
- line: Time-based trends (e.g., monthly hiring over time)
- area: Time-based cumulative trends
- pie: Part-to-whole proportions (e.g., employee distribution by region)
- scatter: Correlation between two numeric variables
- heatmap: Two-dimensional categorical cross-tabulation
- data_table: Detailed row-level data or complex multi-column breakdowns
- funnel: Sequential process stages

AXIS MAPPING RULES:
- For GROUP BY queries: x_axis = the grouped dimension column, y_axis = the aggregated measure
- For time-series: x_axis = the time column/alias, y_axis = the measured value
- For KPI/scalar: x_axis = null, y_axis = null, chart_type = "kpi_card"
- For scatter: x_axis = first numeric column, y_axis = second numeric column

PRIORITY RULES:
- Priority 1: The single most important KPI query (analytical_purpose: "kpi")
- Priority 2: Supporting trend queries (analytical_purpose: "trend")
- Priority 3: Segmentation/breakdown queries (analytical_purpose: "breakdown" or "composition")
- Priority 4: Anomaly detection (analytical_purpose: "anomaly")
- Priority 5: Benchmark comparisons (analytical_purpose: "comparison")
